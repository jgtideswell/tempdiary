Legal & GDPR

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

TempDiary (“we”, “our”) provides digital food safety compliance services for UK food businesses. This policy outlines how we handle your data under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Account holder email address and trading business name.
  • Compliance Logs: Temperature records, equipment labels, opening/closing timestamps, and staff sign-off names or initials.
  • Billing Details: Payment processing is handled exclusively by Stripe. We do not store credit card numbers on our servers.

3. Purpose of Processing

We process your information strictly to maintain compliance records under Food Standards Agency (FSA) regulations, generate Environmental Health Officer (EHO) inspection packs, manage subscriptions, and provide customer support.

4. Data Retention

FSA guidelines typically mandate retaining food hygiene records for audit purposes. Temperature logs and sign-off records are retained securely during your active account tenure and can be exported as PDF dossiers at any time.

5. Third-Party Processors

We use trusted enterprise infrastructure to power TempDiary:

  • Supabase: Encrypted database and user authentication.
  • Cloudflare: Secure edge hosting, DDoS protection, and DNS.
  • Stripe: Payment facilitation and subscription management.
  • Google Analytics: Anonymous web traffic and conversion metrics.

6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data. For requests or inquiries, email support@tempdiary.co.uk.

We use strictly essential cookies and local storage to manage your kitchen login sessions and remember staff names. We also use privacy-focused analytics to improve TempDiary.
